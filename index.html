Six things we do differently

Each one exists because a regular milk shop usually skips it. This is the actual list, not marketing filler.

01

Sourced from one farm cluster

All milk comes from a fixed group of small farms within 15 km, not a mixed tanker supply. You can ask which farm supplied today's batch and get a real answer.

Traceable sourceLocal farms only
02

Tested before it leaves the dairy

Every batch is checked for fat content, adulteration, and bacterial load before packing. Results are logged, not just eyeballed by a supervisor.

Fat % checkAdulteration testDaily log
03

Delivered within four hours of milking

Cows are milked around 2 AM, and delivery runs finish by 6:30 AM. Nothing sits in a tank overnight waiting for a bigger batch.

Same-day milkingCold chain kept
04

Subscriptions you can actually change

Pause for a travel week, skip a single day, or bump quantity for a festival — all from a two-minute call or message, adjusted before the next delivery cycle.

Pause anytimeChange quantity
05

Returnable glass and steel containers

Milk is delivered in reusable glass bottles or steel cans that are collected, washed, and reused, instead of a fresh plastic packet every single day.

Reusable bottlesLess plastic waste
06

One number, one person, real answers

A single phone line goes to someone who actually knows the delivery schedule and farm rotation, not a call centre reading a script.

Direct contactNo call centre

A single delivery day

What actually happens between the farm and your doorstep, hour by hour.

2:00 AM

Milking begins

Cows across the farm cluster are milked and the milk is chilled immediately at the collection point.

3:15 AM

Collected and tested

Cans reach the dairy, where fat content and quality checks happen before anything is packed.

4:00 AM

Packed into bottles and cans

Orders are filled into returnable containers according to each subscription's route.

5:00 AM

Delivery routes leave

Each rider carries one street's worth of orders, so nothing waits long once it's out of cold storage.

6:30 AM

At your door

Last bottle delivered, and yesterday's empties are collected on the same round.
